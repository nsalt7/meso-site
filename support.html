Meso Support

Need a hand with Meso? Email support@mesohypertrophy.com and we will get back to you. Bug reports, feature ideas, and questions are all welcome. If you are reporting a problem, tell us your iPhone model, your iOS version, and what you were doing when it happened.

Common questions

  • How does the free trial work? Your first mesocycle is free. After that, Meso Pro unlocks longer programs and continued training, as a monthly or annual subscription or a one-time lifetime purchase. The price shows in your local currency in the app before you buy, and you are never charged for the first block.
  • How do I restore Meso Pro on a new phone or after reinstalling? Open the app, go to Settings, and tap Restore purchases. Your Meso Pro access, subscription or lifetime, is tied to your Apple Account, so it comes back once restored.
  • How do I manage or cancel my subscription? Subscriptions are billed and managed by Apple, not by us. On your iPhone, open Settings, tap your name at the top, tap Subscriptions, then Meso, where you can change or cancel it. A lifetime purchase is paid once and never renews, so there is nothing to cancel.
  • How do I back up my training or move it to a new phone? Meso stores your data only on your device, so make a backup before you delete the app or change phones. In Settings, tap Back up my data to save a file (to Files, iCloud Drive, AirDrop or email), then use Restore from a backup file on the new device to load it.
  • How does training with a friend work? Both of you need Meso on an iPhone, close together, with Wi-Fi and Bluetooth on. One of you taps With a friend on the Train tab and chooses Share my workout; the other taps With a friend and chooses Join a friend, then accepts. You each log your own sets and see the other's as they land. Lifts you both do count toward your own progress; their extra lifts are logged without changing your program. If it can't connect, check that Local Network access is allowed for Meso in the iPhone Settings app.
  • How do challenges work? Each month has a target sized to your plan, and every lift where you beat last week's best set counts toward it. Special days like Halloween and Christmas Day are earned with one finished session. Your badges live on the Performance tab, and you can turn challenge reminders on or off in Settings.
  • Is my data private? Yes. Meso is local-first. There is no account and no server of ours, and your training data stays on your device unless you export a backup file yourself or share a workout with a friend nearby. See our privacy policy below.
  • Who is Meso for? Adults training for muscle growth, in a gym, at home, or with bodyweight only. See our training readiness note below for guidance on age and getting started.
