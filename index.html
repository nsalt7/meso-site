How it decides

Five rules at the core, and the research behind them

  1. Performance is the acceleratorBeat last week's best sets on a muscle and it earns one more weekly set. Stall and it holds. Regress and it drops.Mann 2010 · Helms 2018 · Enes 2024
  2. Soreness and pain are only brakesSoreness tracks damage and novelty, not growth, so it can hold volume back or cut it, but it never earns more. A lift you mark Painful holds its weight instead of climbing.Damas 2016 · Damas 2018
  3. Reps first, then loadClimb the rep window, then add roughly 2.5 to 5 percent. Go heavier than asked and hold it across every set, and that becomes your working weight.Plotkin 2022
  4. Failure is rationedSets ramp from three reps in reserve down to one. Only isolation work touches failure, only in the last hard week, and never on a lift you can't bail out of.Robinson 2024 · Refalo 2023 · Zourdos 2016
  5. Deloads come early when the data says soTwo weeks of regression across several muscles pulls the deload forward, because a sustained performance drop is the validated overreaching signal.Bell 2023 · Meeusen 2013
